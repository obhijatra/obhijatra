# Trademark policy

> **Draft — to be confirmed.** The name "Obhijatra" is a working name. No trademark is registered yet, and a trademark search is planned before public launch. See `OWNERSHIP.md`.

## What you may do

- Use the name "Obhijatra" for **unmodified** builds of this software.

## What you may not do

- If you change the software, **rename your build**.
- Do not put "certified", "official build" or "hosting partner" next to the name unless you hold a current certification from the project.

## If the project is abandoned

If all three tests in `GOVERNANCE.md` are met (no release for 12 months, no reply to security reports, no reply to the steering group), the steering group may, after a public consultation by The Commons Conservancy, authorise one fork to use the name, logo and domain. The original maintainer may rejoin under the fork's governance. The Conservancy's three-month hibernation clock starts at month nine.

This depends on the Conservancy agreeing to hold the name, which is not yet confirmed.
